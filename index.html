Clean, eBay-ready product photos in one step

prep-listing-photo removes the background from your product shots, adds a pure white backdrop and a subtle drop shadow, and centres each item on a 2000 x 2000 square JPEG.

View the source

What it does

  • Removes the background from flat-lay photos, usually clothing on a floor or rug.
  • Replaces the background with pure white (#FFFFFF).
  • Adds a soft, faint drop shadow beneath the item.
  • Centres the item with a 6% margin on a 2000 x 2000 canvas.
  • Turns upside-down shots 180 degrees before the shadow is drawn.

How to use it

  1. Install the skill. Copy the folder into your skills directory, keeping SKILL.md at its root.
  2. Install the cutout tool. 
    pip install --break-system-packages -q "rembg[cpu]"
  3. Ask for it. Attach your photos and say something like "prep this photo for eBay".
  4. Download the results. Upload the front shot first, as it becomes the main listing photo.

Customise it

  • Shadow: change opacity (0.28 by default) or blur, or set SHADOW = False.
  • Size and margin: change S and pad.
  • Transparent PNG: save the cutout with its mask as alpha instead of pasting on white.
